## FAQ Section for Website

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is this app officially affiliated with Community of Christ?**
A: No, this is an independent project created by a member to serve the CoC community. It's not officially endorsed by Community of Christ headquarters.

**Q: Is it really free?**
A: Yes, 100% free with no ads, no subscriptions, and no hidden costs. It runs on free hosting tiers.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: No account required. Your notes and highlights are saved on your device, so you can start using it immediately.

**Q: Does it work offline?**
A: Yes! Once you visit a chapter, it's cached for offline reading. Perfect for commutes or areas with poor internet.

**Q: What about mobile apps?**
A: Currently web-only, but it works great on mobile browsers. Native iOS and Android apps are planned based on user demand.

**Q: Which editions of the scriptures are used?**
A: Book of Mormon: 1908 CoC edition with original 1830 chapters. D&C: Current CoC numbering (sections 1-167). Bible: Inspired Version references.

**Q: Can I suggest features?**
A: Absolutely! Click the "Feedback" button in the app or email [your email].

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. Notes and highlights stay on your device. No tracking, no analytics, no data collection.

**Q: Can I use this for group study?**
A: Yes! Share your screen or send links to specific verses. Group study features (shared notes, etc.) may come in the future.

**Q: How accurate are the scriptures?**
A: All texts are carefully transcribed from official CoC editions. If you find any errors, please report them via the feedback form.
