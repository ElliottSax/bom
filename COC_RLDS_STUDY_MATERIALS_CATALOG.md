## Theological Differences Summary

### 1. Priesthood and Ordination

**CoC Position:**
- **Women's Ordination:** Since 1984 (Section 156 of D&C)
  - All priesthood offices open to women
  - Based on spiritual gifts and calling, not automatic
  - Ordination by pastoral discernment
- **Priesthood Orders:**
  - Aaronic: Deacon, Teacher, Priest
  - Melchisedec: Elder, Seventy, High Priest, Bishop, Apostle, President/Prophet
- **Process:** Not automatic; based on giftedness and calling

**LDS Position:**
- Priesthood restricted to men
- Automatic progression for worthy males

**Resources:**
- Background Essay: https://sites.smith.edu/womens-rites/season-1/background-essay-womens-ordination-in-community-of-christ/
- Wikipedia Comparison: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparison_of_the_Community_of_Christ_and_the_Church_of_Jesus_Christ_of_Latter-day_Saints
- LDS Living article: https://www.ldsliving.com/what-i-learned-about-women-and-the-priesthood-when-talking-to-members-of-the-community-of-christ/s/92132

**Integration Recommendation:** Include explanatory notes when discussing priesthood in CoC context

### 2. Sacraments and Ordinances

**CoC Recognizes Eight Sacraments:**
1. **Baptism** - By full immersion, age 8+
2. **Confirmation** - Recognizes Holy Spirit's work, follows baptism by any Christian minister
3. **Blessing of Children**
4. **Lord's Supper (Communion)** - Open to all believers, bread and grape juice
5. **Ordination** - To priesthood
6. **Marriage**
7. **Evangelist Blessing**
8. **Administration to the Sick**

**LDS Ordinances:**
- Baptism for the dead (proxy work)
- Temple endowment
- Sealing ordinances
- **Not practiced in CoC**

**CoC Position on Communion:**
- Open table - all believers welcome, regardless of membership
- Grape juice and bread
- Regular congregational celebration

**Resource:** https://cofchristindy.org/discover-who-we-are/sacraments/

**Integration Recommendation:** HIGH - Essential difference; clarify in any liturgical discussions

### 3. Temple Theology

**Kirtland Temple** (Now owned by LDS Church as of March 2024)
- **Historical CoC Use:** Meetinghouse and education center
- **LDS Perspective:** Sacred site where Jesus literally appeared
- **CoC Interpretation:** Spiritual experiences rather than literal physical appearances
- **No secret ordinances** when under CoC ownership

**Independence Temple** (Currently owned by CoC)
- **Dedication:** "Dedicated to the pursuit of peace"
- **Daily Prayer for Peace:** 1:00 PM daily in sanctuary
- **Open to All:** No private ceremonies, public access
- **Sacraments Performed:** Communion, administration to sick, ordination
- **No temple work for the dead**

**Key Differences from LDS:**
- No baptism for the dead
- No endowment ceremonies
- No eternal sealing of families
- No temple recommends required
- Educational and worship focus, not ordinance focus

**Resources:**
- Kirtland Temple: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirtland_Temple
- Independence Temple: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Temple
- BYU Universe article: https://universe.byu.edu/2018/03/27/kirtland-temple-holds-significance-for-lds-community-of-christ1/

**Integration Recommendation:** MEDIUM - Important for understanding CoC vs LDS eschatology

### 4. Progressive Christianity and Theology

**CoC Theological Stance:**
- **Progressive Christianity:** Emphasis on questions, doubt, and belonging over belief
- **Peace and Justice:** Central to mission and identity
- **Social Justice:** Active in poverty abolition, peace work
- **Environmental Stewardship:** Part of mission initiatives
- **Member of:** National Council of Churches (USA), World Council of Churches

**Key Documents:**
- Peace and Justice page: https://cofchrist.org/peace-and-justice/
- Dialogue Journal article: https://www.dialoguejournal.com/articles/community-of-christ-an-american-progressive-christianity-with-mormonism-as-an-option/

**Theological Differences from LDS:**
- More traditional Trinity conception (not three separate beings)
- Rejection of exaltation theology and plan of salvation (LDS version)
- Religious liberalism
- Open to LGBTQ+ inclusion
- Women's ordination

**Integration Recommendation:** HIGH - Central to understanding modern CoC identity

### 5. Continuing Revelation

**CoC Understanding:**
- **Active Process:** Revelation continues through prophetic leadership
- **Sections 114-167:** Modern revelations from:
  - Joseph Smith III (1860-1914): Sections 114-131
  - Frederick M. Smith (1914-1946): Sections 132-138
  - Israel A. Smith (1946-1958): Sections 139-144
  - W. Wallace Smith (1958-1978): Sections 145-152
  - Wallace B. Smith (1978-1996): Sections 153-160 (including Section 156 - women's ordination)
  - W. Grant McMurray (1996-2004): Sections 161-162
  - Stephen M. Veazey (2005-2025): Sections 163-165

**Controversial Sections:**
- **Section 156 (1984):** Women's ordination - caused significant schism
- **Sections after 144:** Not accepted by fundamentalist RLDS branches

**Governance:**
- **World Conference:** Highest legislative body, meets tri-annually
- **Common Consent:** Delegates vote on revelations
- **First Presidency:** Prophet-President + 2 counselors
- **Council of Twelve Apostles**
- **Theocratic Democracy**

**Resources:**
- First Presidency: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Presidency_(Community_of_Christ)
- World Conference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Conference_(Community_of_Christ)
- Priesthood structure: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priesthood_(Community_of_Christ)

**Integration Recommendation:** CRITICAL - Must include sections 114-167 with context

### 6. Historical Context: 1860 Succession Crisis

**Background:**
- Joseph Smith died June 27, 1844
- Initial contenders: Sidney Rigdon, Brigham Young, James Strang
- **Joseph Smith III:** Accepted presidency April 6, 1860 at Amboy, Illinois
- Founded "Reorganized Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints"

**RLDS Claims:**
- Lineal succession through Joseph Smith's family
- Joseph Smith III "ordained and anointed" January 17, 1844 by father and Hyrum Smith
- Rejected polygamy (claimed Joseph Smith never taught it)
- Rejected Utah church's authority

**Key Historical Resources:**
- Succession Crisis: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Succession_crisis_(Latter_Day_Saints)
- Joseph Smith III biography: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Smith_III
- Roger Launius blog: https://launiusr.wordpress.com/2015/02/20/joseph-smith-iii-and-lineal-succession-claims-to-the-mormon-presidency/

**Integration Recommendation:** MEDIUM - Provides essential context for understanding RLDS/CoC identity

```sql
-- Add CoC theological resources table

```
