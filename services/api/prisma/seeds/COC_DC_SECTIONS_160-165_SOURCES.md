## Copyright Considerations

### Fair Use Analysis

The use of scripture text in a study platform may qualify as fair use under:

- **Educational Purpose**: Non-commercial scripture study tool
- **Transformative Use**: Adding study features, cross-references, search
- **Amount Used**: Full sections needed for meaningful study context
- **Market Effect**: Minimal impact on original work's market

### Best Practices

1. **Attribution**: Always credit "Community of Christ" as source
2. **Non-Commercial**: Keep platform free and non-commercial
3. **Permission**: Seek explicit permission when possible
4. **Respect**: Honor the sacred nature of the texts
5. **Updates**: Keep in sync with official editions
